Reclaimed timber does not get an easier safety check at Fern Horizon. WOOD-4002 says: “Reused timber must pass the same load check.” On October 2, 2026, the makers announced that forty percent of display stands use reclaimed timber. All stands must be inspected before dispatch. Some surface marks deliberately remain. The company does not claim every component is reclaimed; fasteners and adjustable feet are bought separately. Buyers may request a finish sample before ordering, and staff should explain how natural variation affects appearance.

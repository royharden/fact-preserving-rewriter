Repair records must accompany all work at Mint River, including repairs using donated parts. The cooperative announced a 900-dollar allowance on October 25, 2026. TOOL-9025 states: “A repair record should travel with the tool.” Members who fail to attach service notes must return the tool to the desk before borrowing again. Cosmetic changes alone are excluded from funding. Some tools may be retired when safe repair is impractical; the desk will explain this to the reporting member so the catalogue has no unexplained gap.

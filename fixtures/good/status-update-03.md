Only reviewed scans can enter the public index: ARC-3007 records the instruction “Only reviewed scans belong in the public index.” At Birch Beacon, 3,000,000 scanned pages are covered by the archive project, and fifty percent had been indexed as of October 15, 2026. A legibility check is still required for half of the folders before publication. Staff must not guess missing words from nearby prose.

Ochre Mill no longer accepts containers whose internal coatings are loose. The shop announced on October 24, 2026, that sixty percent of returned containers can be washed on site. WASH-6024 states: “A clean container must also be completely dry.” All accepted containers require visual inspection before washing. Some narrow-based shapes still need external cleaning because equipment cannot reach inside. Reuse is not promised for every return. Before offering replacement, staff should explain why a container was rejected.

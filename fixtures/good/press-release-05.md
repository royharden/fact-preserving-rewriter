Quartz Orchard's compatibility promise remains unchanged in v4.2.0, released on October 17, 2026. The SDK gains seven examples. In REL-4217, the maintainer says: “Examples should show the failure path as clearly as success.” All examples must work with documented permissions; none need a customer's private workspace. Some demonstrate cancellation, others retrying after temporary failure. Readers should choose the example for their task rather than stitch together unrelated fragments into an undemonstrated workflow.

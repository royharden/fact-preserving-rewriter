A maintenance plan is required for every grant from Aster Foundry's new fund: NEWS-3001 quotes the chair saying, “Every grant must include a maintenance plan.” Announced on October 1, 2026, the fund provides 3,000,000 dollars for refurbishing shared workshop equipment. All applicants must explain continued community access. Private purchases are excluded, and some applications may need clarification before review. Reasons for awards will be published after acceptance. Eligibility does not guarantee funding; workshop stewards are asked to consult regular users about repairs before applying.

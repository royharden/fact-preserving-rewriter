Before ordering the remaining map sleeves, Juniper Signal staff should test a sample against the thickest map. Some fabric-backed maps fold less tightly than paper copies, and trimming either type has not been approved. On October 11, 2026, the library team approved 240 dollars for replacements. LIB-2411 specifies: “The folded maps must stay visible through the sleeve.” Keeping a rejected sleeve beside the sample lets another buyer understand the rejection without repeating the comparison.

The refill shop [[e:Ochre Mill]] announced on [[d:October 24, 2026]] that [[n:60 per cent]] of its container returns can now be washed on site. Notice [[v:WASH-6024]] includes [[q:“A clean container must also be completely dry.”]] The shop no longer accepts containers with loose internal coatings. All accepted containers must pass a visual inspection before entering the wash area. Some shapes still need external cleaning because the equipment cannot reach their narrow bases. The announcement does not promise that every returned container will be reused. Staff should explain a rejection before offering a replacement so that customers understand the reason for the restriction.

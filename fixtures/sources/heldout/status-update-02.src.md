During the packaging pilot, [[e:Moss Parcel]] assigned [[n:twelve]] reusable crates to the repair desk. Inspection takes [[n:5 to 7 minutes]] per crate, and the next review is [[d:next Friday]]. The desk lead added [[q:“Repair the hinge before replacing the shell.”]] to [[v:PKG-8821]]. Staff should keep damaged crates apart from finished stock until inspection is complete. Most returns need a hinge adjustment rather than a new shell. The pilot has not changed the packing instructions given to customers. A sketch beside the workbench shows where to look for wear, making it easier for unfamiliar staff to follow the inspection sequence.

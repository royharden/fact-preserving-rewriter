The team at [[e:Lumen Loom]] completed the accessibility audit on [[d:October 12, 2026]]. The approved budget remains [[n:$1.2M]], and [[n:50%]] of the navigation screens now pass the keyboard review. The release owner recorded [[q:“We must keep the focus indicator visible.”]] in [[v:UX-2048]]. All remaining screens must receive the same review before launch. The team did not remove the older navigation path, because some customers still depend on its familiar layout. Feedback from the pilot is being grouped by task rather than by customer so that a loud complaint does not outweigh a repeated obstacle.

The workshop team at [[e:Orchard Gauge]] no longer uses the shared sanding bench for painted panels. An inspection on [[d:October 14, 2026]] found that [[n:20%]] of the dust bags needed replacement. The safety card [[v:SAFE-1420]] says [[q:“Keep painted work away from the clean bench.”]] All replacement bags must be fitted before sanding resumes. The clean bench remains reserved for untreated material, and the room steward has not approved an exception. New signs point visitors toward the appropriate storage rack. Staff should describe the restriction when handing over the room, since the rack labels alone do not explain why the separation matters.

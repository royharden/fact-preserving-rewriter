During the incident at [[e:Copper Meadow]], the intake queue paused for [[n:9 minutes]] on [[d:October 3, 2026]]. The responder attached [[q:“The queue resumed after the worker was restarted.”]] to [[v:INC-0903]]. All incoming requests remained on disk, and the team did not ask customers to submit them again. Staff must verify the queue length before restarting another worker. Most delayed requests completed without manual intervention, while some needed an explicit retry because their acknowledgement had expired. The review will focus on why the pause was invisible on the ordinary dashboard rather than treating the worker restart as a permanent fix.

The research group [[e:Flint Garden]] introduced its open notebook on [[d:October 10, 2026]]. Editor Dr. [[e:Mira Vale]] approved [[n:16]] illustrated entries for the launch edition. The announcement [[v:NOTE-1610]] states [[q:“An uncertain observation should remain visibly uncertain.”]] All contributors must distinguish direct observation from an explanation they have inferred. The notebook does not rank contributors by the number of entries they submit. Some entries include failed trials because those records may help another group avoid repeating the same mistake. Readers can suggest a correction, but the original entry will remain visible alongside the editor's explanation of any revision.

At [[e:Birch Beacon]], the archive project now covers [[n:3 million]] scanned pages. The search team reports that [[n:50 per cent]] of the pages have been indexed as of [[d:October 15, 2026]]. The checklist in [[v:ARC-3007]] says [[q:“Only reviewed scans belong in the public index.”]] [[n:Half of the folders]] still require a legibility check before publication. Staff must not infer missing words from the surrounding paragraph. Most damaged originals remain in the protected reading room, where volunteers can compare a scan with the paper copy. The project manager is keeping a separate queue for pages whose margins contain handwritten notes.

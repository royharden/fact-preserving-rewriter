The cooperative [[e:Aster Foundry]] announced a [[n:$3 million]] fund on [[d:October 1, 2026]] to refurbish shared workshop equipment. Its release [[v:NEWS-3001]] quotes the chair: [[q:“Every grant must include a maintenance plan.”]] All applicants must describe how repaired equipment will remain available to community users. The fund does not cover private purchases, and some applications may be returned for clarification before review. The cooperative will publish reasons for awards after recipients accept the terms. It is not promising an award to every eligible applicant. The announcement asks workshop stewards to discuss repair needs with regular users before preparing a request.

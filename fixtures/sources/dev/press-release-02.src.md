The makers at [[e:Fern Horizon]] announced on [[d:October 2, 2026]] that [[n:40%]] of their display stands now use reclaimed timber. The announcement [[v:WOOD-4002]] includes [[q:“Reused timber must pass the same load check.”]] All stands must be inspected before leaving the workshop, and the reclaimed material does not receive a lighter safety check. Some surface marks remain visible by design. The company is not claiming that every component is reclaimed, because the fasteners and adjustable feet are purchased separately. Buyers may ask for a finish sample before placing an order, and staff should explain how natural variation affects the appearance of the final stand.

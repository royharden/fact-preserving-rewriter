The library team at [[e:Juniper Signal]] approved [[n:$240]] for replacement map sleeves on [[d:October 11, 2026]]. The purchase note [[v:LIB-2411]] includes [[q:“The folded maps must stay visible through the sleeve.”]] Staff should compare a sample sleeve with the thickest map before ordering the remainder. Some maps have a fabric backing and do not fold as tightly as the paper copies. The team has not approved trimming either kind of map to fit. A rejected sleeve should remain beside the sample so that another buyer can see the reason for rejection without repeating the same comparison from scratch.

The tool cooperative [[e:Mint River]] announced a [[n:$900]] repair allowance on [[d:October 25, 2026]]. Its member notice [[v:TOOL-9025]] states [[q:“A repair record should travel with the tool.”]] Members who fail to attach the service note must return the tool to the desk before borrowing again. All repairs must be recorded even when a replacement part was donated. The allowance does not pay for cosmetic changes alone, and some damaged tools may be retired if safe repair is impractical. The desk will explain that decision to the member who reported the damage rather than leaving an unexplained gap in the borrowing catalogue.
